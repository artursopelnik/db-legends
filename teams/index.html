Dragon Ball Legends Team Guide

The top teams of the current meta, ranked from strongest down: three core fighters, bench options and equipment tips for each team.

Updated: November 2, 2026

🏆 Best team November 2026

#1 🔀 Fusion Warrior

The strongest team right now. LEGEND Super Vegito and LF Goku & Vegeta can end a fight in one long combo, while ULTRA Half-Corrupted Fusion Zamasu holds the line. The tag is deep – the Gogetas and Vegitos all slot in.

Core team

  • LEGEND Super VegitoDamage
  • ULTRA Half-Corrupted Fusion ZamasuDefense
  • LF Goku & VegetaDamage

Bench

ULTRA Super Saiyan God SS Gogeta · ULTRA Super Saiyan God SS Vegito · LF Vegito

Equipment tips

Fusion Warrior tag equips on the whole team: attack sets on Vegito and Goku & Vegeta, health and damage reduction on Zamasu.

#2 👨‍👦 Son Family

The deepest pool in the game. ULTRA Beast Gohan, ULTRA Super Full Power Saiyan 4 Goku and LF Goku & Vegeta form the core, and the tag's Z-Abilities cover health, strike defense and blast damage.

Core team

  • ULTRA Beast GohanDamage
  • ULTRA Super Full Power Saiyan 4 GokuDamage
  • LF Goku & VegetaDamage

Bench

LF Ultra Instinct -Sign- Goku · LF Super Saiyan 2 Gohan (Youth) · LF Super Saiyan 2 Goku

Equipment tips

Son Family tag equips; blast-damage sets match the Z-Abilities, plus strike defense on your front fighter.

#3 🍬 Majin Buu Saga

LEGEND Super Vegito leads, backed by ULTRA Ultimate Gohan Absorbed Buu: Super and LF Super Saiyan 2 Goku. ULTRA Super Saiyan 3 & Super Saiyan 2 Goku & Vegeta is the backbone of the bench.

Core team

  • LEGEND Super VegitoDamage
  • ULTRA Ultimate Gohan Absorbed Buu: SuperDamage
  • LF Super Saiyan 2 GokuDamage

Bench

ULTRA Super Saiyan 3 & Super Saiyan 2 Goku & Vegeta · ULTRA Majin Vegeta · LF Super Saiyan 3 Gotenks

Equipment tips

Majin Buu Saga tag equips on all three; attack sets on Vegito and Gohan Buu.

#4 🧬 Hybrid Saiyan

ULTRA Beast Gohan, LF Super Saiyan Gohan & Trunks (Youth) and SP Super Trunks. From the bench, LF Super Saiyan 2 Gohan (Youth) gains extra damage and damage guard while another Hybrid Saiyan is on the field, and allies keep a damage buff after he revives.

Core team

  • ULTRA Beast GohanDamage
  • LF Super Saiyan Gohan & Trunks (Youth)Damage
  • SP Super TrunksSupport

Bench

LF Super Saiyan 2 Gohan (Youth) · LEGEND Super Saiyan Trunks (Teen) & Gohan

Equipment tips

Hybrid Saiyan tag equips; damage sets on Beast Gohan, health on Gohan & Trunks.

#5 ⏳ Future

A build around ULTRA Half-Corrupted Fusion Zamasu with ULTRA Perfect Cell and LF Super Saiyan Gohan & Trunks (Youth). Future Trunks and Vegito Blue fit as well, and a Zenkai Sparking Zamasu works as a budget slot.

Core team

  • ULTRA Half-Corrupted Fusion ZamasuDefense
  • ULTRA Perfect CellDamage
  • LF Super Saiyan Gohan & Trunks (Youth)Damage

Bench

LF Fusion Zamasu · LF Goku Black · ULTRA Super Saiyan God SS Vegito

Equipment tips

Future tag equips; defensive sets on Zamasu, damage sets on Cell.

#6 ✨ God Ki

A broad tag that covers much of the Super-era top end: ULTRA Half-Corrupted Fusion Zamasu, LF Super Saiyan God SS Vegeta and LF Ultra Instinct -Sign- Goku. The Blue Gogetas, Vegito Blue and the Kaioken Gokus all fit.

Core team

  • ULTRA Half-Corrupted Fusion ZamasuDefense
  • LF Super Saiyan God SS VegetaDamage
  • LF Ultra Instinct -Sign- GokuDamage

Bench

LEGEND Super Saiyan God SS Kaioken Goku · ULTRA Super Saiyan God SS Gogeta · ULTRA Super Saiyan God SS Vegito

Equipment tips

God Ki tag equips; damage sets on Vegeta and Goku, health and damage reduction on Zamasu.

#7 👑 Lineage of Evil

ULTRA Final Form Cooler leads: he lands strong Rising Rushes and wins Dokabaki Impacts. LEGEND Android #17 & Golden Frieza and ULTRA Golden Frieza keep the buffs running.

Core team

  • ULTRA Final Form CoolerDamage
  • LEGEND Android #17 & Golden FriezaSupport
  • ULTRA Golden FriezaDamage

Bench

LEGEND Broly: Fury · SP Final Form Frieza: Full Power

Equipment tips

Lineage of Evil tag equips; attack sets on Cooler and Golden Frieza, health on #17 & Frieza.

#8 🐉 GT

Two win conditions in one team: ULTRA Super Full Power Saiyan 4 Goku and LF Super Saiyan 4 Gogeta both carry the damage, while SP Majuub rounds out the core.

Core team

  • ULTRA Super Full Power Saiyan 4 GokuDamage
  • LF Super Saiyan 4 GogetaDamage
  • SP MajuubSupport

Bench

LF Super Saiyan 4 Vegeta

Equipment tips

GT tag equips; attack sets on Goku and Gogeta, health on Majuub.

#9 🎬 Sagas From the Movies

ULTRA Final Form Cooler and LEGEND Broly: Fury bring the damage, SP Android #13 supports. A strong option if you have Cooler but not the top fusions.

Core team

  • ULTRA Final Form CoolerDamage
  • LEGEND Broly: FuryDamage
  • SP Android #13Support

Bench

SP Super Saiyan Broly · SP Turles · SP Super Saiyan 3 Goku

Equipment tips

Sagas From the Movies tag equips; attack sets on Cooler and Broly.

#10 💀 Powerful Opponent

LEGEND Android #17 & Golden Frieza leads, with ULTRA Final Form Cooler and LEGEND Broly: Fury as the damage dealers. A good alternative if you own Cooler and Broly but not the top fusions.

Core team

  • LEGEND Android #17 & Golden FriezaSupport
  • ULTRA Final Form CoolerDamage
  • LEGEND Broly: FuryDamage

Bench

SP Super Saiyan Broly · SP Turles · SP Super Saiyan 3 Goku

Equipment tips

Powerful Opponent tag equips; attack sets on Cooler and Broly, health on #17 & Frieza.

The meta shifts with every new banner. Treat these builds as a proven starting point, not as the only option. Character names follow the English game version.
