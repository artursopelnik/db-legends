Dragon Ball Legends Team Guide

The strongest teams of the current meta, hand-curated: three core fighters, bench options and equipment tips for every top tag.

Updated: August 6, 2026

🔀 Fusion Warrior

The poster team of the current meta: ULTRA SSGSS Gogeta delivers enormous strike damage while both Zamasu builds add healing and punish careless switches. The Z-Abilities line up almost perfectly, so the core works even without the newest banner units.

Core team

  • ULTRA Super Saiyan God SS GogetaDamage
  • LF Fused Zamasu (Red)Defense
  • LF Half-Corrupted Fusion ZamasuSupport

Bench

LF Super Saiyan 4 Gogeta · LF Vegito Blue · SP Super Gogeta (Red)

Equipment tips

Pure strike-attack equips on Gogeta, a mixed attack/defense set on Fused Zamasu and one health-focused equip on the support slot – Fusion Warrior tag equips give the biggest flat boosts.

✨ God Ki

Combines the two strongest attackers in the game – ULTRA SSGSS Gogeta and ULTRA Ultra Instinct Goku – with Fused Zamasu as a durable anchor. Massive burst damage with enough sustain to recover from a lost exchange.

Core team

  • ULTRA Super Saiyan God SS GogetaDamage
  • ULTRA Ultra Instinct GokuDamage
  • LF Fused Zamasu (Red)Defense

Bench

LF SSGSS Vegito · LF Super Saiyan God Goku · SP God of Destruction Beerus

Equipment tips

Strike equips on both Ultras, one God Ki tag equip for the big flat boosts, and a defensive health set on Zamasu for the late game.

👨‍👦 Son Family

The classic all-rounder: huge health pools, strong strike defense and big damage multipliers thanks to perfectly aligned Z-Abilities. Beast Gohan and UI Goku carry the damage, Youth Gohan holds the defensive slot.

Core team

  • ULTRA Ultra Instinct GokuDamage
  • ULTRA Beast GohanDamage
  • LF Super Saiyan 2 Youth GohanDefense

Bench

LF Bardock · SP Super Saiyan God Goku · SP Super Saiyan Goten (Kid)

Equipment tips

Blast-attack equips on Beast Gohan, strike on UI Goku, and health/strike-defense equips on Youth Gohan – Son Family tag equips play into the team's naturally high health.

♻️ Regeneration

Wins the long game: Janemba, Fusion Zamasu and Majin Buu heal every turn and simply outlast burst-oriented teams. Ideal if you like defensive, patient PvP.

Core team

  • ULTRA JanembaDamage
  • LF Half-Corrupted Fusion ZamasuSupport
  • LF Majin Buu: GoodDefense

Bench

LF Super Baby 2 · SP Orange Piccolo · SP Perfect Cell (Revived)

Equipment tips

Health and defense first: stack health-percentage equips on the whole core – Regeneration healing scales with max health – plus one damage equip on Janemba.

⚡ Hybrid Saiyan

Hybrid Saiyans hit fast and often: Beast Gohan and Gohan & Trunks chain combos while Youth Gohan covers defense. A strong pick if you pulled the 8th-anniversary units.

Core team

  • ULTRA Beast GohanDamage
  • LF Gohan & Trunks (Adult)Damage
  • LF Super Saiyan 2 Youth GohanDefense

Bench

SP Super Saiyan Trunks (Kid) · SP Super Saiyan Goten (Kid) · LF Super Trunks

Equipment tips

Split by main stat: blast equips for Beast Gohan, strike for Gohan & Trunks, plus one Hybrid Saiyan tag equip and a defensive set on the Youth Gohan slot.

🐒 GT

Pure aggression around ULTRA Super Saiyan 4 Goku: SS4 Vegeta and SS4 Gogeta keep up the ki pressure and end matches before sustain teams come online. Simple game plan, very high damage ceiling.

Core team

  • ULTRA Super Saiyan 4 GokuDamage
  • LF Super Saiyan 4 VegetaDefense
  • LF Super Saiyan 4 GogetaDamage

Bench

SP Pan (GT) · SP Super Baby 2 · SP Majuub

Equipment tips

Full aggression: strike-attack equips on SS4 Goku and Gogeta, a mixed set on Vegeta, and one GT tag equip – here damage wins before sustain matters.

The meta shifts with every new banner – treat these builds as a proven starting point, not as the only option. Character names follow the English game version.
